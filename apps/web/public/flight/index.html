Tip for iPhone: pick the file from Browse or Files, not straight from Photos. Phones remove the location before a website can see it. The steps are below.

See yours in 4 steps

IPHONEOpen the flight video in Photos, tap Share, then Options at the top. Turn on All Photos Data, tap Done, then Save to Files. Come back here, tap the yellow button, choose Browse and pick that file.
ANDROIDTap the yellow button and pick the video from Files or Browse. If it shows nothing, your camera didn’t save the location: Camera, Settings, Location tags.
NOTHING FOUND?Videos sent on WhatsApp or downloaded from Instagram lose this data. Use the original from your own phone.

Want to turn it off?

IPHONE, NEW PHOTOSSettings, Privacy & Security, Location Services, Camera, Never.
IPHONE, WHEN YOU SHAREIn the Share sheet tap Options and turn off Location.
ANDROIDOpen Camera, Settings, and switch off Location tags.
