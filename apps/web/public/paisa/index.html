Add your UPI or bank statement. See where your salary went, and what to cut.

Your statement never leaves your phone. It is read right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or saved.

How to get your statement

  1. Open the PhonePe app and tap History.
  2. Tap Download Statement at the top of the screen.
  3. Pick the period (last 30, 90 or 365 days, or your own dates).
  4. Tap Proceed and save the PDF. Then choose it here.

If the PDF asks for a password, it is usually your 10-digit PhonePe mobile number.

  1. Log in to HDFC Bank NetBanking or the mobile banking app.
  2. Open your savings account and find Account Statement.
  3. Choose the dates and download it as PDF (or Excel, then save as CSV).
  4. Choose the file here.

Bank PDFs often have a password. Check the email or SMS from your bank for the format.

  1. Any bank: download your statement as CSV or PDF from net banking.
  2. Paytm: Balance & History, the three dots, then Download UPI Statement. Pick Excel and save it as CSV.
  3. Google Pay: See transaction history, More, then Get Statement (PDF).

PhonePe and HDFC Bank PDFs are read best. Other bank PDFs work too, but may be less accurate. A CSV with Date and Amount columns always works.
